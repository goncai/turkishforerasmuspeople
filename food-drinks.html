🥗 Dietary & Allergy Phrases

Turkish cuisine uses meat, nuts and dairy widely. Learn these before eating out to stay safe and comfortable.

Vejeteryanım.
I am vegetarian.
Veganım.
I am vegan.
Yer fıstığı alerjim var.
I have a peanut allergy. (fıstık alone often means pistachio)
Gluten yiyemiyorum.
I cannot eat gluten.
İçinde et var mı?
Does it contain meat?
İçinde süt ürünü var mı?
Does it contain dairy?
Domuz eti yemiyorum.
I don't eat pork.
Buna alerjim var.
I am allergic to this.

🌟 Cultural Notes — Eating Out in Turkey

🍵
Çay after the meal
Many restaurants offer a glass of çay after the meal for free, and your glass may be refilled without asking. In tea houses (çay ocağı, çay bahçesi) you pay per glass. Declining is fine — just say 'Yok, teşekkür ederim' or put your spoon across the glass.
💰
Tipping (bahşiş)
Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory. Around 5–10% is common in restaurants. Rounding up the bill (e.g. paying ₺100 for a ₺87 meal) is very common and perfectly acceptable.
🤝
Ismarlama — treating someone
If someone says 'Ben ısmarlıyorum' (I'll treat you), do not fight over it — accept gracefully. Treating friends is a strong cultural gesture and refusing can feel offensive.
🧾
Splitting the bill (ayrı hesap)
Asking for a separate bill is unusual in Turkey — it can seem cold or stingy among friends. Usually one person pays and others treat next time. Among students it's more flexible.

🎭 Real-Life Dialogue — At the Restaurant

Ordering a full meal — from arrival to paying the bill

🧑‍🍳
Hoş geldiniz! Ne alırsınız?
Welcome! What would you like?
🎓
Menüyü alabilir miyim?
Can I get the menu?
🧑‍🍳
Buyurun, tabii. İçecek ister misiniz?
Of course, here you go. Would you like a drink?
🎓
Bir su ve bir çay alabilir miyim? Ayrıca bu acılı mı?
Can I get a water and a tea? Also, is this spicy?
🧑‍🍳
Çok az acılı. Acısız yapalım mı?
It's a little spicy. Shall we make it non-spicy?
🎓
Evet, lütfen. Ve bir mercimek çorbası da istiyorum.
Yes please. And I'd also like a lentil soup.
🧑‍🍳
Tabii, hemen getiriyorum.
Of course, I'll bring it right away.
🎓
Teşekkürler. Hesabı alabilir miyim?
Thank you. Can I get the bill?
🧑‍🍳
Hemen getiriyorum. Afiyet olsun!
Right away. Enjoy your meal!
