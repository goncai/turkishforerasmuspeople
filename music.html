Song 1: Manifest - Snap
Kaçma, kovalamam (kaçma, kovalamam)

Don't run, I won't chase you (don't run, I won't chase you).
Senin gibisini çok (çok) çok (çok) çok (çok) gördüm

I've seen so, so, so many like you.
Affedemem, hayır (affedemem, hayır) sesin gelmiyo' hiç

I can't forgive you, no — your voice doesn't reach me at all.
Yok, yok, yok, bye-bye (bye-bye)

No, no, no, bye-bye.
Sana alkış yok, anca finger snap-nap-nap-nap-nap

No applause for you, only a finger snap.
Manifest is a six-member girl group. They are currently among the most popular artists in Türkiye. During your Erasmus experience, it is highly likely that you will hear their songs at parties, social events, and various entertainment settings. Their music is especially popular among younger audiences.
Song 2: Tarkan - Hüp
Kuş sütüyle beslerim seni

I would take great care of you. (lit. "I'd feed you with bird's milk" — something impossibly rare)
Mis yerine koklarım seni

I would cherish you deeply. (lit. "I'd smell you like a sweet fragrance")
Kalbimin sarayları senin

The palaces of my heart belong to you.
Sen ağlat ben severim seni

Even if you make me cry, I still love you.
Tarkan is a legendary Turkish pop artist who has remained popular since the 1990s and continues to produce music today. He appeals to all generations, including young people, middle-aged audiences, and older listeners. In almost any social setting in Türkiye, you are very likely to hear his songs, as he has produced many widely recognized hits.
