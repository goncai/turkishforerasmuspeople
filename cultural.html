🌟 Everyday Cultural Expressions

Afiyet olsun
Enjoy your meal
Kolay gelsin
Hope it goes easy — said to someone working
Geçmiş olsun
Get well soon / sorry for your trouble
Hoş geldin
Welcome
Hoş bulduk
Response to welcome
Eline sağlık
Well done / Good job (lit. health to your hands)
